# NV-211 / NV-212 (Claude B, 2026-10-25), Thangseng answers relayed by the PO
1. **Uachi / Iachi in negative motion:** "iachi and uachi will not take the negative suffix. It should be affixed to the verb." Negative go/come clauses are directional like the affirmative: `I do not go there` = Anga uachi re·angja; `he did not come here` = Ua iachi re·ba·ja; `I will not go there` = Anga uachi re·jawa. Static verbs keep bare `uano` / `iano` (NV-209).
   **Spelling fix:** the native form is `Uachi` (master_dictionary verified_high: `there (towards/that side)`, `Uachi on·tisa re·angbo`; Thangseng wrote "uachi"). The engine had `Uchi` from the unverified NV-200 submission; it now emits `Uachi`. Left for Claude A: dictionary rows `to that place` / `there (direction)` = `Uchi` (unverified) still use the old spelling; `uchipak` unchecked.
2. **`Look at that dog.` = `Ua achakko nibo`:** that = Ua, noun takes -ko, verb last. Generalised to `look at that NOUN` -> `Ua NOUNko nibo` (`look at that house` = Ua nokko nibo). Bare `look at that` -> `Uko nibo` (derived from attested uko, not a native quote; Thangseng did not rule on it). `look at this dog` and other demonstratives are unchanged.
