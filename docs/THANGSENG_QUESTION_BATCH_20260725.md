## 2. Optional follow-up on register/distribution (low priority)
Not a gap, just unexplored: the response introduced a fourth "want"
word, `nangnika` (from "do you want the book?" = `Na'a ki'tapko
nangnikama?`), alongside the already-confirmed `ska`/`sikeng`. Worth
asking someday whether `nangnika` is interchangeable with `ska`, or
has its own register/context (e.g. wanting an object vs. wanting to do
something) — not urgent, translation works fine with the existing
words either way.
