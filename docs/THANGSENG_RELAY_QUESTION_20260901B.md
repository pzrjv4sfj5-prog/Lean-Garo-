## SEND THIS SECTION TO THANGSENG — nothing else in this file

**1. Ability / "can"**
We already know `ama` = "can."
- How do you say "I can speak Garo"?
- Where does the word for "can" normally come in the sentence?
- Can you give one or two more examples with "can"?

**2. Counting people**
We already know `Sak` is the word used for counting people.
- Is that different from the word used when counting other things
  (like animals or objects)?
- Please give one example counting people and one example counting an
  object.

**3. More than one adjective**
- How do you say "big red house" in Garo?
- When a noun has two describing words, such as "big" and "red," what
  order do they come in?
- Please give the natural Garo sentence.

**4. Purpose / "to do something"**
- How do you say "I went to eat" / "I went to work" in Garo?
- How is the meaning "to eat," "to work," or "in order to ..."
  normally expressed?
- Please give one or two natural examples.

**5. Questions with a question word**
- When you make a question using a question word such as "who,"
  "what," "where," or "which," is a question ending also used?
- Please give a natural example.
- Are there situations where you would not use a question ending with
  a question word?

**6. Do these two sentences sound right?**
1. "Anga chattro·ko mangmang" — meant to say "I am the only student."
2. "Angade te·ga·chu Bitekosan Cha·aia" — meant to say "the only fruit
   I eat is mango."
If either sounds wrong, could you say it the correct way?
