A concrete, citation-backed lead worth Thangseng's attention
specifically: master_dictionary.json already has a separately-tagged
entry *"Us. (in the accusative case)."* → *An·chingko* (idx nearby
*"Us; to us."* → *An·chingna*), distinct from the plain *"us"* →

same *root-changes-for-accusative* shape as *Angko* (me) and *uko*
(him/her/it), not a bare-pronoun-plus-suffix shape — so *An·chingko*,
not *Chingna·ko*, may be the actually-correct object form for "us."
Included question 4 above so Thangseng's own natural sentence settles
it directly rather than us guessing from the dictionary entry alone.

No equivalent accusative-tagged entry exists anywhere in
